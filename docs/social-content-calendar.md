# NaijaArtisans — 2-Week Social Media Content Calendar

Ready-to-post content for launching NaijaArtisans across Nigerian social media.
Link to share everywhere: **https://naijaartisans.com**

**Tips**
- On Instagram/TikTok, put the link in your **bio** (captions can't be clicked).
- Post WhatsApp Status daily — it's the highest-reach free channel in Nigeria.
- Best times: 7–9am, 12–1pm, 7–10pm (WAT).
- Re-share each post in 2–3 local Facebook/WhatsApp groups.
- Swap the trade/city in each post to match where you want customers.
