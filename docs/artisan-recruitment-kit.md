## Where to recruit in Lagos (go where artisans already are)
- **Associations/unions:** mechanic villages (e.g. Ladipo), tailoring guilds,
  electricians' associations, plumbers' unions — one leader = many members.
- **Market & cluster spots:** Computer Village (device repair), Alaba (electrical),
  furniture/woodwork clusters, fashion hubs.
- **Online groups:** Facebook "Lagos Buy & Sell" and handyman/artisan groups,
  estate WhatsApp groups, Nairaland Services section.
- **Estate & gate networks:** estate managers know every handyman in the area.

## Best practice
- Lead with the **free** benefit and **more jobs** — that's what they care about.
- Always get consent before collecting anyone's details.
- Focus on **one city (Lagos)** and a few **trades** first — depth beats spread.
- Aim for **5–10 active artisans per popular trade** before pushing customer ads.
