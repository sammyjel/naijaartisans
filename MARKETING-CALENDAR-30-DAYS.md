# NaijaArtisans — 30-Day Social Media Calendar

**Goal:** build awareness + recruit artisans + drive traffic to naijaartisans.com
**Platforms:** Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), TikTok
**Tool:** schedule everything in **Metricool** (free plan). Connect each account once via its own login (never share passwords).

---

## How to use this file
1. In Metricool, open **Planner**.
2. For each day below, copy the caption for the platform, attach the matching image, set the date/time, click **Schedule**.
3. Reuse the 4 Canva templates we made + your own real photos of artisans at work (real photos always beat graphics — ask artisans to send you "before/after" shots).
4. Do it in one sitting and the whole month posts itself.

## Best posting times (Nigeria / WAT)
- **Facebook:** 12:00pm & 8:00pm
- **Instagram:** 1:00pm & 7:30pm
- **X (Twitter):** 8:00am & 6:00pm
- **TikTok:** 7:00pm – 10:00pm

## Posting rhythm
You don't need every platform every day. A sustainable mix:
- **Facebook:** 5x/week · **Instagram:** 5x/week · **X:** daily (it's fast) · **TikTok:** 3x/week (video)
