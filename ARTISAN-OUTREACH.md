# NaijaArtisans — Artisan Outreach Kit

Goal: get real artisans to register at **naijaartisans.com/register?role=artisan** — legally, without a field agent, mostly automated.

---

## THE MACHINE (set up once, runs on autopilot) — recommended

### Facebook/Instagram → WhatsApp ad
This is the closest thing to a "robot" that is legal and actually converts. You pay a small budget; Facebook shows your ad to thousands of Nigerian artisans; anyone interested taps a button that opens a WhatsApp chat with you (or auto-reply), and you send them the registration link.

**Why this beats scraping + SMS:**
- Reaches thousands you could never find manually
- 100% opt-in (they tap to message you) = legal + no bans
- Runs 24/7 with no agent
- You only "talk" to people who already raised their hand

**Setup (once):**
1. Create a **Facebook Page** for NaijaArtisans (if not already).
2. Set up **WhatsApp Business** (free app) on the business phone number.
3. In Facebook Ads Manager → Create Ad → Objective: **Engagement → WhatsApp** (or "Send Message").
4. Targeting: Location = Nigeria (or start with Lagos + Abuja). Interests = "home improvement, electrician, plumbing, tailoring, small business, entrepreneurship." Age 24–55.
5. Budget: start tiny — **₦3,000–₦10,000 total** over 5–7 days to test.
6. Use the ad copy below. Button: **Send WhatsApp Message.**
7. Set a WhatsApp Business **greeting message** (auto-reply) so every new chat instantly gets your link.

> Welcome to NaijaArtisans 🛠️🇳🇬
> Thanks for reaching out! List your service **FREE** and get customers near you — no fee.
> Register in 2 mins 👉 naijaartisans.com/register?role=artisan

### C. Jiji chat message (when YOU message an artisan you found on Jiji)
Keep it short — Jiji is for business contact, so this is legitimate outreach (an invitation, not auto-signup):
> Hello 👋 I saw your [TRADE] listing on Jiji. I run **NaijaArtisans**, a free platform where customers across Nigeria find artisans like you. Would you like a **FREE** listing? Takes 2 mins, no fee: naijaartisans.com/register?role=artisan 🎁 First artisans get 30 days Featured free.

### D. WhatsApp broadcast — ONLY to artisans who saved your number / opted in
(Never to cold/scraped numbers.)
> Hi 👋 Quick one — NaijaArtisans is live! List your service FREE and get customers near you: naijaartisans.com/register?role=artisan. Need help registering? Just reply. 🛠️🇳🇬

### E. Pidgin version (great for trust)
> Oga/Madam 👋 I see your work for Jiji. **NaijaArtisans** na free platform wey dey connect you to customers for all Nigeria. E free to list — no fee. Register sharp sharp (2 mins): naijaartisans.com/register?role=artisan 🎁 First artisans dey get 30 days Featured free.

## FREE PATH YOU CAN START TODAY (manual, but zero cost)
No budget yet? Do this yourself, ~30 mins/day:
1. **Jiji:** search a trade + city (e.g. "AC repair Lagos"). Open 15–20 listings.
2. Use their **Jiji chat** (or the business number they publicly posted) to send **Message C** above.
3. Log each one in `artisan-outreach-tracker.csv`.
4. **Facebook groups:** search "Lagos artisans", "Nigeria electricians", trade unions. Post **Message A**. Join 10+ groups.
5. Follow up with anyone who replies; walk them through registering.

**Target:** 20 contacts/day → even a 15% join rate = 3 real artisans/day = ~20/week.

---

## RULES (so you never get banned or fined)
- ✅ DO: message artisans one-to-one on Jiji/Facebook where they posted to be contacted.
- ✅ DO: broadcast only to people who messaged you or saved your number.
- ✅ DO: run WhatsApp/Facebook ads (opt-in, unlimited scale).
- ❌ DON'T: scrape numbers into a list and mass-text them (spam, DND-blocked, illegal, bannable).
- ❌ DON'T: register anyone without their consent.
